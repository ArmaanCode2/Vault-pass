Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VaultPass and its offline security model.

Can you recover my master password if I forget it?

No. VaultPass employs a zero-knowledge, zero-backdoor architecture. Your vault is encrypted using AES-GCM with keys derived via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 from your master password. Because no servers or recovery keys exist, lost master passwords cannot be reset by anyone.

How does the Android Autofill feature work?

VaultPass integrates directly with Android's system Autofill framework. When an app or website presents a login form, VaultPass uses Breadth-First Search (BFS) DOM traversal to safely identify input fields and match credentials based on domain, app label, and package heuristics.

How do I backup my credentials without cloud servers?

VaultPass provides an encrypted backup utility using the .vpex format. Backups are encrypted with Base64 AES-GCM, allowing you to safely store them on a flash drive, local hard drive, or your personal storage medium. Plain JSON and TXT exports are also supported.

What happens if an unauthorized person tries brute-forcing my PIN/Password?

The application enforces strict hardware-persisted brute-force protection. Progressive lockout cooldowns trigger at 5, 10, 15, and 20+ failed attempts (30s, 60s, 5m, and 15m lockouts), effectively halting automated guessing attacks.
